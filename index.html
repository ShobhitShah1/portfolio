About me

I like mobile products that feel calm, useful, and obvious from the first screen.

I work mainly in React Native and TypeScript, but the work is never only about writing code. I spend most of my time turning rough product ideas into screens that feel lighter, clearer, and easier to trust in daily use.

Most of the apps here were built under real delivery pressure, so the role has always been a mix of UI craft, implementation, debugging, integration, and shipping.

How I think I prefer simple structure, strong spacing, clear hierarchy, and interactions that do not get in the user's way.
How I work I usually move between product thinking, visual cleanup, API integration, QA fixes, and release polish in the same cycle.

What I usually shape

UI systems Component consistency, visual rhythm, and cleaner screen structure.
Product flows Onboarding, scheduling, editing, payments, and content-driven mobile journeys.
Release quality Edge cases, stability work, integration cleanup, and final production readiness.
Mobile feel Motion, density, readability, and details that make an app feel more finished.
